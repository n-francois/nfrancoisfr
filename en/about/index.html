Background

For over 20 years, I’ve been supporting organizations through their digital transformation. As Digital Director at Agence Régionale du Tourisme Grand Est, I lead Explore Grand Est (opens in a new tab), a platform that brings together 2,200+ partners, recognized at the UX Awards and the Trophées de la Communication Digitale.

I also know tourism from a less visible angle: data. Information systems, DataTourisme, structuring tourism content. This is where destinations become discoverable, at a time when travelers increasingly turn to AI for answers.

Based in France, I work at the crossroads of AI and tourism, bringing a European perspective to an industry largely shaped by North American narratives. Since 2024, I’ve been testing AI for tourism and sharing what I learn in IA, Tech & Travel Café (opens in a new tab), my independent media outlet, and on LinkedIn (opens in a new tab), both in French. No jargon, no magic promises.

I believe AI is a powerful lever for the industry, provided we approach it with method, critical thinking, and a bit of humility.

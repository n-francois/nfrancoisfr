Background

I started my career writing code: six years as a developer and web designer, then as a project manager and UX designer. I’ve since moved into strategy, but I’ve kept one habit: testing tools myself before talking about them.

I moved into tourism in 2013, joining Lorraine Tourisme. Today, I head the digital department at Agence Régionale du Tourisme Grand Est and coordinate Explore Grand Est (opens in a new tab), a platform that brings together 2,200+ partners and has been recognized at the UX Awards and the Trophées de la Communication Digitale.

I also know tourism from its hidden side: data. Information systems, DataTourisme, structuring tourism content… That’s where destinations win or lose visibility, now that travelers put their questions to AI.

Since 2024, I’ve been sharing what I test in IA, Tech & Travel Café (opens in a new tab), my independent media outlet, and on LinkedIn (opens in a new tab), both in French. I’ve also spoken about it on stage more than twenty times so far, from Next Tourisme to Grands Remous in Québec. Talks are delivered in French.
